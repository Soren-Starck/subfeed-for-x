First public release.

- **Never twice** hides the posts you have already read, and anything older than 72 hours, from X's Home timeline. Posts are removed before X draws them, so nothing flashes.
- **Minimalist** hides X's news, trends, suggestions and footer, and leaves Home, Notifications, Profile and Post in the left menu.

To install, download `subfeed-for-x-0.5.0.zip` below, unzip it, and load the folder with **Load unpacked** on your browser's extensions page (Developer mode on). The [README](https://github.com/Soren-Starck/subfeed-for-x#install) has the steps.
